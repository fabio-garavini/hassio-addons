- **More reliable reading and listening**: Publisher font sizes now follow the web reader's text-size setting, non-English dictionary lookups work again, and more EPUBs open successfully. Audiobook tracks stay in disc and filename order, and Book Dock measures durations so newly filed audio can record progress immediately. <!-- icon: BookOpen -->

- **Find and request the right edition (iOS 1.5)**: Filter Library and Search by language, request both ebook and audiobook together, and add a missing format to an existing book. Metadata search results identify saved answers and offer a fresh search when connected to a supporting server. <!-- icon: Languages -->

- **Smoother reading across phone, iPad, and Watch (iOS 1.5)**: Orbit Voices playback works again after the voice service changed. Watch audiobook progress sync is restored, iPad readers retain their page layout and panels after backgrounding, and book details stay smooth during downloads. <!-- icon: RefreshCw -->

- **Read Along that moves with you (iOS 1.5)**: Continue narration in the mini-player while browsing, then return to the same passage. A compact listening footer brings playback, speed, sentence steps, and sleep controls together; browse ahead and use Back to the voice to return. An optional setting keeps the ebook reader's screen awake. <!-- icon: BookAudio -->
