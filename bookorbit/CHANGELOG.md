- **Customizable sidebar dock, command palette, and unified browse controls**: The web app replaces its global header with a sidebar dock, a Command-K palette, consistent page titles, and one compact browse command bar for libraries, smart scopes, collections, authors, and series. Hide or reorder sidebar rows and groups with drag, keyboard controls, or Undo, and search books, people, places, settings, and actions without leaving the keyboard. <!-- icon: Command -->

- **DjVu web reader with OCR text**: Scanned DjVu books now import with covers and page counts, open in the image reader, expose embedded OCR text, and can be sent by email. Decoding is bounded and cached for large libraries. <!-- icon: FileImage -->

- **Kobo delivery, position, annotation, and cover fixes**: Book replacements, first downloads, read-along positions, bookmarks, annotations, notebooks, covers, store cursors, sync tokens, encoded chapter paths, and mapped ports are handled more reliably. Full-size covers are enabled by default, and Kobo reading time no longer counts twice. <!-- icon: BookOpenCheck -->

- **Correctable author matches and broader book requests**: Edit author facts, choose and pin the right provider record, and keep later refreshes tied to that match. Request searches can browse more results, rerun by exact author, and submit unmatched titles; releases can link to safe source pages and be held for review at any score. <!-- icon: SearchCheck -->

- **In-place download updates and resumable transfers (iOS)**: Replace outdated downloads while the existing copy stays readable, and resume interrupted comics. Downloads remain isolated by account, and Apple Watch warns when its audiobook copy is outdated. Uploads retry with new sessions; Book Requests stay current and offer only permitted actions. <!-- icon: Download -->

- **Cross-device narration, active chapters, and Read Along recovery (iOS)**: Narration changes prompt before replacing a paused or playing position, with silent catch-up and Undo from accessories. Read Along streams audio, survives WebKit restarts, resumes at the correct text, and appears in CarPlay. Audiobook chapter lists open at the active chapter, while Siri resumes the last title heard. <!-- icon: Headphones -->

- **PDF, comic, directional reading, and annotation fixes (iOS)**: Continuous PDFs save the page being read, comics recover without jumping, and vertical and right-to-left books page correctly. Squiggly highlights, accessible style icons, sibling-edition marks, and unplaced notebook annotations expand the reading tools. <!-- icon: Highlighter -->
